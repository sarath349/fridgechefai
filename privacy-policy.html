Privacy Policy for RecipeGenie

Last updated: September 17, 2026

RecipeGenie (“the App”) helps users discover recipes from ingredients they already have. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle and how it is used.

1. Information we store on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device (for example using on-device storage):

  • Saved / favorite recipes
  • Dietary preferences
  • User preferences such as servings
  • Onboarding / first-launch flags

This information stays on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall the App.

2. Information you may choose to provide

To generate recipe suggestions, you may optionally:

  • Type ingredient names
  • Take a photo or choose an image of ingredients (camera / photo library permission)

When you use these features, ingredient text and/or images may be sent over a secure connection (HTTPS) to third-party recipe or AI services so the App can return results. We do not use this content for advertising.

3. Account / sign-in

The App does not currently require account creation or sign-in.

4. Ads and sale of data

The App does not show ads and does not sell your personal information.

5. Permissions

  • Camera / Photos: only to capture or select ingredient images when you choose that feature
  • Internet: to fetch recipes and related content from online services

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local data can be removed by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. Data sent to third-party APIs is processed to provide the requested feature and is subject to those providers’ policies.

8. Third-party services

The App may use third-party APIs (for example recipe databases or AI services) to generate results. Those services process requests under their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
