# Privacy Policy for FridgeChef AI

**Last updated: [DATE]**

## Introduction

FridgeChef AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Ingredients and Recipes**: Text input of ingredients and generated recipes
- **Photos**: Images of ingredients you choose to upload for recipe generation
- **Preferences**: Dietary preferences, cooking preferences, and app settings

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app (features used, time spent)
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Log Data**: Error logs and performance data to improve the app

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- **Generate Recipes**: Process your ingredients and dietary preferences to suggest recipes
- **Improve AI**: Enhance our recipe generation algorithms and image recognition
- **Provide Support**: Respond to your questions and provide customer service
- **App Improvement**: Analyze usage patterns to improve app functionality

## Third-Party Services

### Spoonacular API
- **Purpose**: Recipe search and generation
- **Data Shared**: Ingredient lists and dietary preferences
- **Privacy**: Subject to Spoonacular's privacy policy

### Google Vision API (Optional)
- **Purpose**: Image recognition for ingredient identification
- **Data Shared**: Photos you choose to upload
- **Privacy**: Subject to Google's privacy policy

### Image Processing
- **Local Processing**: Images are processed locally when possible
- **Cloud Processing**: Some images may be processed by third-party AI services
- **Data Retention**: Images are not permanently stored on our servers

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- **Recipe Favorites**: Stored locally on your device
- **User Preferences**: Stored locally on your device
- **Images**: Temporarily processed, not permanently stored

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data transmitted using HTTPS encryption
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal information
- **Regular Updates**: Security measures regularly updated

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- **View Data**: You can view your stored preferences in the app
- **Delete Data**: You can delete saved recipes and preferences
- **Opt-out**: You can disable image recognition features

### Data Portability
- **Export**: You can export your saved recipes
- **Delete Account**: Contact us to delete your data

## Children's Privacy

FridgeChef AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
